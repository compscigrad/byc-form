Applications Now Open

We are building a speaker lineup, wellness track, liberation workshops, and Twelve Step meeting schedule for the 2026 BYC National Conference — and we need community members to make it happen. Below you will find every open application, what each role requires, and a direct link to apply.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Submitting early gives us the most time to build a balanced, representative program.

Open Application — 3 Slots Available

Conference Speakers

Personal Testimony & Recovery Story

Conference speakers are the heart of our main programming. Each speaker shares a personal testimony — their experience, strength, and hope — in a dedicated session open to all conference attendees. Three speaker slots are available for the 2026 conference.

Applicants must submit a 20–30 minute audio recording of themselves speaking as part of the application. Recordings are reviewed by the conference programming committee.

Requirement Audio recording · Personal recovery story

Open Application — Facilitator Positions

Liberation Workshop Facilitators

Step-Based · Black Queer Recovery Experience

The Liberation Sessions are Step-based workshops centering the lived experience of Black queer people in recovery. We are seeking qualified facilitators to lead these sessions — spaces that are honest, grounded, and community-rooted.

Facilitators must demonstrate solid experience and knowledge of the Twelve Steps. These are not lecture-style presentations — they are participatory, recovery-centered workshops.

Requirement 12-Step knowledge · Lived recovery experience
Facilitate a Liberation Workshop →

Open Application — Facilitator Positions

Wellness Workshop Facilitators

Holistic Health · Community Healing · Recovery

Our wellness track brings together facilitators with professional backgrounds in holistic health, mental wellness, financial literacy, movement, breathwork, grief work, and community care. We are looking for qualified practitioners who can lead engaging, recovery-affirming sessions.

All applicants must demonstrate a commitment to sobriety and wellness. Workshop proposals should have a clear format, structure, and intended outcome for attendees.

Requirement Professional qualifications · Sobriety commitment
