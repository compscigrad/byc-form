We are building a speaker lineup, wellness track, liberation workshops, and Twelve Step meeting schedule for the 2026 BYC National Conference — and we need community members to make it happen. Below you will find every open application, what each role requires, and a direct link to apply.

Open Application — 3 Slots Available

Conference Speakers

Personal Testimony & Recovery Story

Conference speakers are the heart of our main programming. Each speaker holds a dedicated session open to all conference attendees — sharing a personal testimony rooted in their experience, strength, and hope. Three speaker slots are available for the 2026 conference.

To apply, you must submit a 20–30 minute audio recording of yourself speaking. Recordings are reviewed by the conference programming committee and used to evaluate fit, tone, and alignment with the conference theme.

Open Application — Facilitator Positions

Liberation Workshop Facilitators

Step-Based · Black Queer Recovery Experience

The Liberation Sessions are Step-based workshops centering the lived experience of Black queer people in recovery. We are seeking facilitators who can hold space that is honest, grounded, spiritually rooted, and community-centered.

Applicants must demonstrate solid experience and knowledge of the Twelve Steps. These are participatory workshops — not lectures. Facilitators should be comfortable guiding group discussion, holding vulnerable space, and drawing from their own recovery journey.

Required 12-Step knowledge  ·  Lived recovery experience

Open Application — Facilitator Positions

Wellness Workshop Facilitators

Holistic Health · Community Healing · Recovery

Our wellness track brings together practitioners, healers, and professionals whose work centers recovery, holistic health, and community care. We are looking for facilitators with backgrounds in mental wellness, financial literacy, movement, breathwork, grief work, body confidence, and community health.

All applicants must demonstrate a commitment to sobriety and wellness. Workshop proposals should have a clear format, structure, and an intended outcome — something attendees can carry into their recovery.

Required Professional qualifications  ·  Sobriety commitment

Open Application — 5 Meeting Slots

Chair a 12-Step Meeting

Early Bird · Night Cap · Traditional Format

Throughout the conference weekend we are hosting Early Bird and Night Cap recovery meetings — traditional Twelve Step-style spaces running alongside the main programming. These meetings are separate from the featured conference speakers and open to all attendees.

Meeting chairs may open with a share, guide the group through selected Twelve Step literature, facilitate discussion, and/or open the floor. Format is flexible — we want volunteers who are comfortable holding recovery space in a structured, welcoming way.
